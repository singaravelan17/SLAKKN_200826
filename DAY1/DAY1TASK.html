During my 3rd year of Mechanical Engineering, around the 7th semester, a few of us were hanging out in the corridor below the stairs. We were chatting about all the symposia, hackathons, and codathons happening around. Someone said, "Hey, why don't we just go for fun like a mini tour and get OD (On Duty) permission?" Everyone immediately agreed. While looking for events, we found a poster for a symposium at Kumarasamy College in Karur. We registered as two separate teams. Only after registering did we even go to our HOD for OD approval, and thankfully, with the help of two staff members, our OD got signed. We were so unprepared that we didn't even know our own team names! We spent the entire train journey trying to prepare something. After getting off at the station, we walked about a kilometer along the highway to reach the college. It was already so much fun. We refreshed, had breakfast, and headed to the event venue to set up our project. To be honest, none of us in the team really knew much about the project! We just stood there with confidence. When the chief guests arrived and asked about our project, we gave them the project title and explained everything in simple Tamil as best as we could. Suddenly, they asked for our team name. At that exact second, I had no clue, but one of my teammates randomly blurted out, "Spartans!" And just like that, "Team Spartans" became our official name. By evening, out of around 70 to 80 participating teams, they started announcing the prize winners from 5th place backwards. 5th place went, then 4th, 3rd. At that point, we completely lost hope, thinking we didn't stand a chance. Then came the grand announcement for 2 nd place: "Team Spartans from panimalar Engineering College!" We were completely shocked and couldn't even express how happy we felt! We won a cash prize of ₹7,000. With that prize money in hand, we didn't go straight home—we went straight from Karur to Kodaikanal, had the time of our lives, and then returned with unforgettable memories.
