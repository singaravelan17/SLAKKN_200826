KABBADI-PARAVAKKOTTAI

Paravakkottai is a village where Kabaddi is more than just a sport. Kabaddi has been closely connected with the people and culture of the village. Many young players grow up watching and playing Kabaddi in their local grounds. Kabaddi brings the whole village together with excitement, unity, and pride. The strong bond between Paravakkottai and Kabaddi makes the village special.
